Battle sprites in this folder come from two CraftPix.net freebies. The horse
ridden by every mounted unit is drawn by code in battle.js (Horse); the Battle
for Wesnoth cavalry sprites used until 1.33 are no longer shipped.

infantry_weak.png / infantry_normal.png / infantry_armored.png
  From "Free Swordsman 1-3 Level Pixel Top-Down Sprite Character" —
  https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-swordsman-1-3-level-pixel-top-down-sprite-character-pack/
  One idle frame per level (1/2/3), cropped to content — now only the loading
  placeholder for the animated sprites below.

swordsman_1.png / swordsman_2.png / swordsman_3.png
  The same pack, every animation (idle, walk, run, attack, hurt, death) in all
  four facings, as separate body/head/sword layers so armour and weapon can
  come from different levels. Each layer sheet is cropped to its content and
  packed into one atlas per level by tools/build-swordsman.js; pixels are not
  altered (recolouring and the helmets are drawn at runtime by battle.js).

archer_anim.png
  The same archer, every animation (idle, walk, attack, hurt, death) facing

  tools/build-swordsman.js; pixels unaltered (team colours are dyed at runtime).
