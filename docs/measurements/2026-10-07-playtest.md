# Playtest — early and mid game, 2.11.3 (2026-10-07)

A bug sweep, then the game played as a player: a fresh character for 32 days (Orta, Turkish,
1400×900, sound off), and the 2.9.0 save (`tools/saves/2.9.0.json`, day 47) for 22 more. A scripted
driver clicked the real screens in headless Chromium: it fought "Kolay"/"Dengeli" encounters,
sent its troops when it could, fled anything harder, supported every lord in a clash, bought
food when supplies ran low, and took road events at random. Measurement tools ran alongside it.

## Bugs found and fixed (PR #178, 2.11.3)

1. **Windows locked after leaving an encounter without a fight.** After a flee or a release, Esc and
   × no longer closed anything, and a window with no button of its own never closed (7150995).
2. **Invariants.** Raiding, waiting, besieging and captive each need their own record (4da15db, f7f14a7).
3. **Wolves took captives.** They held you 5 days, took 90% of your purse and asked a ransom. They now
   scatter the party and leave the purse (984b73c). The countdown on the captivity panel never moved
   (same commit).
4. **The tournament wrote its line-up onto the saved bracket.** About a quarter of brackets that
   reached the semi-final could not be saved (1416cbb).
5. **A language switch made inside a town left the town's cards in the old language** (9db2b59).
6. **A pack that beat you jumped the wounded hero again at once,** about 20 defeats in a row with −2
   renown each. This came from fix 3, which removed the captivity pause. The pack is now fed for
   24 hours (bbbb5c9).
7. **Loot can overload the bag with no way out.** Beating 4 mountain bandits that had robbed a
   caravan brought 302 coal. The bag went to 441/75 and speed to ×0.014: 1291 hours to the nearest
   town instead of 18, with −10 morale a day. Nothing could be dropped. The victory screen now says
   so, and an overloaded bag offers **Bırak** on every stack except unique and special items
   (f4c891b). SYSTEMS still listed the old linear overload formula; corrected.

## Balance — what the numbers say

### Early game (days 1–32, a fresh character)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Income from 9 won fights | ~200₺ (each win 7–97₺; "Kolay av" cuts rewards to 20–57%) |
| Income from 3 achievements | **1500₺** (200 + 650 + 650), each a 5 s toast (the bot read only modals and missed them), no sound, no news line |
| One kitchen shift | 93₺ in 4 hours, about 3× a won fight |
| Defeats | 2 full party wipes in a month; one captivity took 1160₺ (90%), another 4712₺ |
| Healing | 1 hp per 8 hours (Vitality 11): about 18 days to full after a defeat; the inn heals fully in 8 hours for 10₺ |
| Lone hero | flee chance 10% against 5 looters; 3 encounters in a row, then captured |
| Day 32 | 74₺, alone, morale 1 |

**Wolves.** They are 24–43% of all bands, with about 34 bands in total. The real engine, 9
villagers + hero against a pack:

| Pack | Result |
|---|---|
| ≤ 5 wolves | 98–100% wins |
| ≥ 6 wolves | the Alfa Kurt joins (55 hp, 112 speed, 15 attack): villagers alone win 0%, with an AI hero 70% |

The 70% matches what "Dengeli" promises, so the label is honest. But the outcome hinges entirely on
the hero. Auto-resolve at the same odds (ratio 1.14, ±15% luck) would win about 90%.
*Correction (during implementation):* the send button needs 1.5× strength, so at 1.14 it is not
offered and "sending beats leading at even odds" does not happen. The real gap is the reward:
where both are offered, sending paid exactly what leading did, for none of the risk, and its
outcome was certain (`won = ratio > 1`).

Without a fief, the 10-man army only lives on band purses.

## Smaller findings

- **Turkish dative suffix is hard-coded** after names and numbers. About 20 sites have `'a`/`'e`
  baked in: `%47'e` (should be `%47'ye`), `Narra'a` (should be `Narra'ya`), `${loc.name}'e`.
  Examples: battle.js:3697, app.js:5505/5655/10891/10928/11571, nobles.js (gift, compliment,
  poem, envoy), quests.js:214/452/481.
- **The top-bar chip "4+4/12"** right after hiring 4 means 4 men, 4 of them new. It reads like 8.
- **Village markets** (e.g. Emirin) sell the Kraliyet Kılıcı (6743₺), war horses and the Boss Haritası.
- **Spending attribute points shows nothing at once.** A point raises a target and the attribute
  trains toward it. *Correction:* the character screen does say so (the "/ N hedef" target, a bar,
  how it trains); only the click itself gave no visible reaction.
- **The arena** starts a bout at current hp, with no warning. At 30/76 the novice floored me and a
  day was lost.
- **A sworn vassal is turned away at the king's hall** for being 1 renown under the 80 gate.
- **The flee message** says "atlarını sürüp uzaklaştın" ("rode away on your horses") for a party
  on foot.
- **The encounter promised** "rewards drop to 20%", and the result said 23%.
- **Day 1 starts with a red "0 gün erzak"** (zero days of food).
- **Road events** (black cat, hail, feverish caravaneer) are flavorful and cheap. They were the most
  fun texture of the run.
- **What works well:**
  - the recovery loop when broke and beaten (kitchen shift → inn rest → recruit);
  - supporting lords in clashes (0 losses, a reward and relation);
  - band cargo you can see before the fight.

## Proposals (phase 3) — not implemented, awaiting a decision

Ranked by how much fun they add for the work. Numbers are starting points to measure, not settled.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A defeat costs less of the run.** Purse loss 90% → ~50%. A beaten hero isn't jumped by any band for ~a day (as the fed pack isn't). Faster healing while resting on the map. | Two wipes in a month plus a 4712₺ loss: one bad roll erases an hour of play, and the lone hero who comes to beside a band loses again |
| 2 | **Leading pays at least as well as sending.** Auto-resolve on the real-engine odds curve (`odds-data.json`) instead of ratio ±15%, and/or a bonus (xp, renown, loot) for fighting yourself. | Sending paid the same as leading, with a certain outcome: the game didn't reward playing its best part (see the correction above) |
| 3 | **The Alfa Kurt cliff.** Let him join at 8+ wolves, or weaken him; and/or wolves ≤ 25% of bands. | 98% → 0% at one extra wolf; wolves are a third of all bands |
| 4 | **A nearer mid-game goal.** A vassal gets a village at ~150 renown; mark quest target bands on the map; quest renown up. | 300 renown is ~250 days at the measured pace; the mid game is grinding small bands for +3 renown |
| 5 | **Arena bouts at full health** (it's practice), or at least the hp shown with a warning. | A wounded hero loses a day and earns nothing |
| 6 | **Achievements announce themselves** (fanfare and a news line beside the toast), and maybe pay less. | 1500₺ of early income arrives as a short toast that is easy to miss |
| 7 | **Trade pays on long routes.** Either accept it (trade as a side activity) or let the margin grow with distance. | −16/day with a 10-man party, the same since September |
| 8 | **A Turkish suffix helper** (`Game.dat(name)`, vowel harmony + buffer y) at the ~20 sites. | Visible grammar errors in every session |
| 9 | **Small UI fixes.** The "+N new" chip; village market stock; attribute-point target shown; the vassal hall gate; the flee text on foot; the day-1 food warning. | Polish |
